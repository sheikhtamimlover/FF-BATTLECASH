## 📱 About FF BATTLECASH

<img align="right" src="assest/homepage.jpg" width="220" />

**FF BATTLECASH** is an Android application and web platform designed specifically for **Free Fire enthusiasts in Bangladesh (BD Zone)**. Turn your Free Fire skills into real rewards!

- 🎮 **Join Tournaments**: Participate in exciting Free Fire matches (BR, CS, Open Challenge) with real prize pools.
- 💰 **Earn Real Money**: Win tournaments and earn actual money directly to your wallet.
- 🌐 **Play Anywhere**: Download the Android app or play on iPhone/PC via our [Web Client](https://ffbattlecash.top/client).
- 🏆 **Competitive Ranking**: Climb the global leaderboard and prove you're the best.
- 💳 **Instant Withdrawal**: Quick and secure payouts via bKash and Nagad.
- 🤖 **AI Tools & Social**: Built-in AI chat, support, and gift sending!
